BRIEFAssumptions, context and the design system I'm working in
WHAT I READ

A ₦2,500/month group protection programme for 500k→1m Nigerian public servants: ₦5m life, ₦5m accidental death, disability, ₦250k accident medical, ₦50k/week temporary disability, funeral and education support. The document's own differentiator is not the cover — it's transparency: a CSP-ID, a digital certificate, and a Track My Claim journey.

WHAT I ASSUMED

One app, three roles (member · beneficiary · HR officer) behind a role switch — these designs show the member. Android-first at 390pt, offline-tolerant, plain language, USSD and WhatsApp surfaced inside the app rather than hidden in a help page. Every naira figure on screen is illustrative and labelled as such, because the product is pre-underwriting.

THE SYSTEM I'M IN

Broadsheet: Source Serif 4 on paper white #f3f2f2, ink #201e1d, process cyan #0088b0 for interaction and magenta #d6006c as the rare second spot. You asked for "modern fintech" — this system gets there through editorial confidence rather than gradients and rounded cards, which suits a government-backed benefit better than a neobank look would.
